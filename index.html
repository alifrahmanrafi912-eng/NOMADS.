About NOMADS

Not a rental.A different way to arrive.

NOMADS is a proposed premium mobile-hospitality concept for Bangladesh. Instead of separate transport and accommodation, it offers one private space on four wheels — a Toyota Hilux-based camper with overnight space for two adults.

It is a business concept prepared for the Department of Marketing, University of Dhaka, with Dhaka as its base city and Cox’s Bazar and Sylhet as the initial proposed routes.

Cash break-even

When monthly rental revenue covers the ≈৳1,20,000 of cash fixed operating cost. At ৳18,000/day that is ৳1,20,000 ÷ ৳18,000 ≈ 6.7 paid days — about 7 days. It counts cash fixed cost only.

Accounting break-even

Where illustrative accounting profit reaches zero. In the term-paper scenarios it falls between 12 days (−৳12,000) and 16 days (+৳54,000) at ৳18,000/day — a higher bar than cash break-even.

Why it matters

Positioning

A premium, intimate alternative to fragmented transport-plus-hotel planning — selling the feeling of a room on the road, not just a vehicle.

Pricing

A ৳15,000–22,000 daily range anchors NOMADS as a premium experience, with ৳18,000 as the working demonstration rate.

Feasibility

Utilisation is the swing factor: moving from 8 to 16 paid days a month shifts the illustrative result from −৳78,000 to +৳54,000. Testing real demand is the key next step.
